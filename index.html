FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

In most cases, I only need the property's existing photographs. I can work with photos you already have, even if they weren't taken professionally. If needed, I can also advise you on how to capture better images for the best possible result.

Every project is tailored to your needs and may include photo review and selection, professional image enhancement, object removal, cinematic video, horizontal and vertical formats, 4K delivery, creative direction, revisions included and content ready for websites, property portals and social media. Website design can also be included if required.

Every project is quoted individually depending on the property's size, complexity and final deliverables. Projects start from €150.

Most projects are delivered within 48–72 hours after receiving all the required material. Larger projects may require additional time, which will always be confirmed in advance.

There is no upfront payment. You only pay once the agreed result has been delivered and you're happy with the final work.
